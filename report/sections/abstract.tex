\begin{abstract}
Reliable answers about medicines must be accurate and grounded in authoritative sources, yet Vietnamese lacks resources for evaluating drug-information retrieval, and practical deployments are often limited to low-cost language models. This paper presents a Vietnamese drug-information corpus derived from the national drug formulary and package leaflets, together with a 10{,}000-question retrieval benchmark. We compare sparse, dense, hybrid and reranked retrieval and report effectiveness alongside cost; hybrid retrieval followed by a mid-sized reranker achieves the best ranking quality (97.5\% Hit@10, 0.859 nDCG@10). We further evaluate an agentic retrieval-augmented generation pipeline designed for low-cost models on a 500-item golden set, using established correctness, attribution and safety metrics, paired ablations and a calibrated automatic judge. \pending{E2E headline finding}. The resources and evaluation protocol provide a reproducible basis for research on Vietnamese medical question answering.

\end{abstract}
