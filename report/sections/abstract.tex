\begin{abstract}
Grounded drug question answering demands both the right source passage and the discipline to refuse when no source supports an answer, and Vietnamese has neither the corpora nor the benchmarks to study it. We build a Vietnamese drug-information corpus from the national drug formulary and package leaflets, a 10{,}000-question retrieval benchmark whose answer locations are known by construction, and a 500-item golden set that covers multi-turn, unanswerable, out-of-scope and prompt-injection requests. On the retrieval benchmark, hybrid fusion followed by reranking places a correct passage in the top ten for 97.5\% of questions and reaches an nDCG@10 of 0.859, raising Hit@3 by 6.4 points over hybrid retrieval alone. On this retriever we build an agentic retrieval-augmented generation pipeline that keeps its control flow in code and asks the language model only for small structured decisions, so that it runs on low-cost hosted models and on the open-weight Qwen3.5-9B. The agentic design changes what the system can answer rather than merely polishing its answers. On conversational follow-up questions, single-step RAG leaves 96\% unanswered at a nugget recall of 0.04, whereas our pipeline answers every one of them with a nugget recall of 0.91 and a truthfulness of 0.84. Over the whole golden set it cuts unanswered questions by 63\%, from 11.9\% to 4.4\%, holds faithfulness at 0.95 with 97\% of its citations supported by the cited passage, and rejects every unanswerable and prompt-injection request without a single successful attack.
