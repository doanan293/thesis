\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

\textbf{Retrieval-augmented and agentic generation.} RAG conditions a generator on retrieved passages to ground knowledge-intensive answers~\cite{lewis2020rag}. Later work lets the pipeline decide when and how to retrieve: Self-RAG trains the model to emit reflection tokens that trigger retrieval and critique its own output~\cite{asai2024selfrag}, Corrective RAG adds a lightweight evaluator that triggers corrective retrieval when the documents are poor~\cite{yan2024crag}, and Adaptive-RAG routes each question to no, single-step or multi-step retrieval according to a predicted complexity~\cite{jeong2024adaptiverag}. These methods either fine-tune models or rely on a capable LLM to plan, whereas our pipeline keeps control flow in code and asks inexpensive models only for small structured decisions.

\textbf{Medical RAG and retrieval benchmarks.} MIRAGE evaluates 41 combinations of corpora, retrievers and LLMs on 7{,}663 English medical questions and shows that the choice of corpus and retriever strongly affects accuracy~\cite{xiong2024medrag}. Mikkelsen benchmarks ten embedding models and BM25 on three clinical corpora with deterministic queries and finds that corpus type, query format and chunking together rival the choice of model~\cite{mikkelsen2026clinical}. Nygren et al.\ answer drug side-effect questions with compact LLMs over a curated resource and report accuracy and latency together~\cite{nygren2026drug}. General retrieval benchmarks such as BEIR~\cite{thakur2021beir} and MTEB~\cite{muennighoff2023mteb}, and its Vietnamese translation VN-MTEB~\cite{pham2025vnmteb}, rank embedding models, but none targets Vietnamese drug information and they do not report the cost of hybrid fusion or reranking.

\textbf{Evaluating RAG systems.} RAGAS scores faithfulness and answer relevancy without references~\cite{es2024ragas}, and ARES calibrates LLM judges with a small human-labelled set through prediction-powered inference~\cite{saadfalcon2024ares,angelopoulos2023ppi}. ALCE measures whether each generated statement is supported by the passages it cites~\cite{gao2023alce}. CRAG scores answers as perfect, acceptable, missing or incorrect so that hallucinations cost more than abstentions~\cite{yang2024cragbench}; the TREC 2024 RAG track checks answers against atomic nuggets~\cite{pradeep2024nuggets}; RGB tests negative rejection when no evidence exists~\cite{chen2024rgb}; and AgentDojo measures how often prompt injections succeed against LLM agents~\cite{debenedetti2024agentdojo}. Reporting guidelines for health-chatbot and LLM studies ask authors to disclose model identifiers, prompting, query strategy and how performance was assessed~\cite{chart2025,gallifant2025tripodllm}. We combine these metrics in one protocol.

\textbf{Vietnamese medical QA.} Vietnamese resources include ViMQ, patient questions annotated for intent and medical entities~\cite{huy2021vimq}; ViHealthQA, 10{,}015 expert-answered health questions used for two-stage BM25 and SBERT retrieval~\cite{nguyen2022spbertqa}; and UIT-ViCoQA for conversational reading comprehension of health news~\cite{luu2021vicoqa}. URAG, presented at SoICT 2024, combines rule-based and hybrid RAG for a university admission chatbot~\cite{nguyen2025urag}. None of these works covers drug labelling or evaluates an agentic RAG system end to end.
